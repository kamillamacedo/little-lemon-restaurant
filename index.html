Traditional Mediterranean recipes, served with a modern twist.

We are a family-owned restaurant bringing the fresh, vibrant flavors of Italian, Greek, and Turkish culture straight to your table. Our menu is intentionally short, focusing on quality and rotating seasonally to ensure every ingredient is served at its peak. Here, tradition meets simplicity.

Seared Feta with Honey & Thyme

A Greek island classic reimagined. We sear premium feta cheese until it is warm and soft on the inside with a crisp, golden crust. Finished with a drizzle of local wildflower honey, walnuts and fresh thyme, it balances savory and sweet flavors in every bite.

Cherry Tomato Confit Gnocchi

Light, pillow-soft potato gnocchi made fresh daily in our kitchen. We toss the pasta in a rustic sauce of cherry tomatoes slow-roasted in olive oil and garlic, completed with genuine parmesan and basil picked from our own garden.

Orange & Pistachio Semolina Cake

Inspired by Turkish baking traditions, this semolina cake is soaked in a light orange and spice syrup right out of the oven to keep it incredibly moist. It is served with a dollop of strained yogurt and toasted pistachios for a perfect crunch.
